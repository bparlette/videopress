# Trust signals — is the channel healthy?

## The test
Upload a boring 3-minute video, wait 72h, check YouTube Studio → Analytics → Reach. Look at browse/suggested impressions (NOT search):
- **300+** healthy
- **500+** strong
- **1000+** blessed
- **<200** cooked — start a new channel, don't try to save it

## Why new channels get 0 views
Almost always channel setup, not content. Two causes:

1. **Too much, too fast.** 5 videos in 3 days on a fresh channel looks like a spam farm; the damage follows every future upload. Fix: 1 video per 48–72h for the first 2 weeks. Once "browse features" shows up in traffic sources, ramp to daily, then 2–3/day with 3h gaps.
2. **Mixing niches.** Romance Monday, WW2 Wednesday, survival Friday — by video 10 YouTube has conflicting data on 3 audiences and serves none. Fix: 1 niche per channel. 3 niches = 3 channels.

## Diagnosis order
Trust → upload pacing (count first-14-day uploads) → niches. Fix the channel before touching the content. Never judge CTR/retention under 3,000–5,000 views — the sample is too small.

## Rescue (stuck but healthy channels only)
- One collab from a healthy channel in the same/nearby niche (observed: 15 → 400+ views in 24h). One collab, not a series.
- Playlists on a healthy channel including the stuck videos (2–4 weeks, stacks).
- End screens from the strong channel pointing at the stuck one.
- Failed trust test = abandon. A new channel gets views faster than fixing a dead one.
