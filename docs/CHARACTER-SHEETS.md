# Character sheets — one face, every video

The internet is full of fake faces. The money goes to the ones that never change.

## Build once
Generate a continuity sheet from 2–3 reference photos:
- Character profile + 4-angle turnaround (front, 3/4, side, back)
- 3 facial close-ups (skin, hair, lips detail)
- 8 expressions, 6 poses
- Costume details + color/material palette
- A **"DO NOT CHANGE"** checklist the model can't argue with

## Use everywhere
Video prompt: "Replace the person in {video} with the person in {character sheet}."
Motion, camera, and audio come from the video. The face comes from the sheet.

## Design rules
- One silhouette people recognize in 1 frame (copper bob, sunglasses, brooch — pick 3 anchors).
- A family around the character, so one face turns into a show.
- Use your own character. Anyone else's is the demo, not the template.

## Cost warning
Bad sheet = wasted tokens. At ~$0.32/sec, every failed 8-sec try burns ~$2.50, and faces start drifting by clip 3. Get the sheet right before generating video.
