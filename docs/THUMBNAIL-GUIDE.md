# Thumbnail guide — CTR is the bottleneck

If YouTube is showing impressions but nobody clicks, the thumbnail and title are the problem — not the channel, not the content.

## Rules
- **2–4 words max.** Bold, readable at 120px wide. Test every thumbnail shrunken before shipping.
- **High contrast.** White or yellow text, thick black stroke, dark scrim behind text.
- **Face/emotion close-up beats text-only.** A reaction face next to the words outperforms words alone.
- **Before/after + red arrow** is the proven pattern for explainers — the brain needs to know how she got there.
- **One text block** + optional smaller sub-line. Never two competing messages.
- **Never** tiny text, busy backgrounds, or more than 4 words.

## Titles (same job as the thumbnail)
- Curiosity gap + trend hook, never a summary.
- Front-load the hook words. Numbers work ("12M Views for Walking?").
- The title and thumbnail must tell ONE story together, not two.

## The 120px test
`videopress thumbnail` reminds you on every run: shrink it to 120px wide. If you can't read it instantly, redo it.
